## How I approach engineering

**Connect the whole workflow.** My projects cover the path from a user's input to validation, API operations, data persistence, and the resulting interface. Football Clubs brings those pieces together through reusable forms and related Django models.

**Make complex work understandable.** NewsLens separates extraction, comparison, and grading into distinct LangGraph stages. That structure makes the responsibilities of each step explicit and gives the application a place to evaluate its output.

**Design around background work.** PulseCheck moves network checks into a worker queue and uses schedules, hooks, and database aggregation to coordinate recurring tasks.
